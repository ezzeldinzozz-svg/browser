Profiles

Each profile has its own history, bookmarks, extensions, cookies, settings and addresses — for example one for work and one for personal browsing. Profiles open in their own windows.

Addresses

Fill in address and contact forms with one click: focus a field like "Name" or "Street" and pick an address. Operecs offers to save one when you send a form. Passwords and cards come from your password-manager extension.

Your data

Save your bookmarks (HTML, works in any browser) and your history, settings and site permissions (JSON) to a folder. Import history from another browser on the History page.

Reset

Restore every setting to its default. Bookmarks, history, passwords in extensions and site data stay.
