Privacy

Operecs has no accounts, no telemetry, no analytics and no crash reporting. Your bookmarks, history, settings and site permissions stay on your computer, in the browser's profile folder.

What goes over the network

Besides the websites you visit, Operecs itself contacts:

  • Update check: GitHub Releases for ezzeldinzozz-svg/operecs-browser, at launch and every 6 hours (or when you click Check for updates). When there's an update, the update file is downloaded from there.
  • Ad and tracker block lists: Ghostery's CDN, on first launch and then about once a week.
  • Your search engine (DuckDuckGo by default): what you type, when you search from the address bar.
  • Search suggestions (off by default): if you turn them on, what you're typing in the address bar goes to your search engine's suggestion service, without cookies and never from private windows.
  • Site icons: fetched from the site you're visiting, without cookies.

These requests include your IP address and a standard browser user agent, like any web request.

Private windows

Private windows keep cookies, site data, history, permissions and the download list in memory only, and erase them when the window closes. Files you download stay on disk.

Third-party cookies and trackers

By default Operecs blocks third-party cookies and known ads and trackers. You can change both in Settings. Sites can ask to use their cookies while embedded; you'll be asked first.
