Notes:
- Class 9 (software) is the class a browser falls in, so openECS shares a class. It is a different
  word (open-ECS, an engineering control system) and reads differently, so a conflict looks
  unlikely, but it is the one to mention to a lawyer.
- Opera (the browser) is a well-known mark in the same field. "Operecs" starts with "Oper-"; keep
  the logo and naming clearly distinct from Opera's (no red O, no "Opera" wording).
- To register: USPTO about $350 per class (class 9, maybe 42); EUIPO from €850 for one class;
  WIPO Madrid to extend to more countries.
