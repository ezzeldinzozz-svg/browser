BUILT DIFFERENT

The Four Pillars of Operecs

We reimagined what a desktop web browser should be when you strip away corporate surveillance and feature creep.

PRIVACY HARDENED

Surveillance-proof by default.

Most browsers track your habits and monetize your attention. Operecs operates on a strict zero-telemetry policy. Everything stays on your physical Mac.

✓
Integrated Ghostery Ad & Tracker Blocker Blocks banner ads, invasive video pre-rolls, and analytics beacons before they hit your CPU.
✓
Automatic Cookie Notice Annihilator Instantly dismisses annoying GDPR cookie popups with zero clicks required.
✓
Fingerprinting Noise Injection Adds imperceptible randomized noise to Canvas, WebGL, and Audio buffers to prevent cross-site identity tracking.
✓
Local-Only SQLite with FTS5 Search Your history and bookmarks are stored in an encrypted local SQLite database. No cloud sync leaks, ever.
PURE SPEED

Instant startup. Zero lag.

Cold launches in under a second. No background cryptocurrency mining, no AI latency overhead, and no bloated tab processes draining your battery.

WORKFLOW

Tailored for deep concentration.

Work the way you think. Seamless vertical tabs that collapse to icons when you need screen estate, native side-by-side split screen, and floating bookmarks.

Collapsible Vertical Tabs

Pin tabs, group workflows, and collapse to a micro-rail with automatic hover expansion.

Native Split View (⌥⌘\)

Inspect documentation on the right while running localhost on the left. Zero window juggling.

Hover-Only Bookmarks

Bookmarks glide into view as a floating overlay on toolbar hover, causing zero layout shift.

Instant Screenshot Tool

Capture Visible Area, Selection, or Full Scrolling Page directly from the toolbar (⇧⌘S).
